Our point of view

Technology delivers more in a well-ordered organisation: that is why we treat organisation, technology, compliance and strategy as a single system

1

Organisation first

A new system applied to muddled processes tends to automate the muddle. Projects start with the structure, the processes and the responsibilities.

2

AI starts with use cases

The starting point is not the tool, it is the problem. We prioritise use cases by expected return and implementation effort.

3

Compliance by design

Data protection, information security and AI governance come in at the start of the project, not as a correction at the end.

4

Prepared people, measured results

Training, change management and indicators monitored by the board consolidate what has been implemented.

Services  ·  01

An organisation that is simpler to manage, with documented processes and clear responsibilities

Organisation and Methods

Organisation and Methods (O&M) consultancy analyses how the business is organised and how the work is done, in order to remove duplication, clarify responsibilities and reduce operating costs. Each engagement starts with a diagnosis and ends with processes, procedures and indicators that the team applies day to day.

Questions we help to answer

  1. Is the structure keeping pace with the company's growth?
  2. Where are time, quality or money being lost in current processes?
  3. Who decides, who carries out and who controls each activity?
  4. Which procedures are needed for a certification or audit?

What it includes

Organisational diagnostic
Organisational architecture: mapping, design and redesign
Process mapping and optimisation, on a digital basis
Internal rules and procedures
Roles, responsibilities and management of areas
Improvement plans and their follow-up
Internal audits of processes and management systems
Support with licensing, accreditations and certifications

Typical deliverables

  • Diagnostic report with priorities
  • Organisation chart and responsibility matrix
  • Current and proposed process maps
  • Manual of rules and procedures
  • Improvement plan with indicators

Services  ·  03

Compliance proportionate to risk protects customers and data subjects without holding back operations

Data protection and compliance

We act as Data Protection Officer (DPO) and support the application of the GDPR, the NIS2 Directive on cybersecurity, DORA on the digital operational resilience of the financial sector, and AI governance, with strategic clarity, operational realism and common-sense governance.

Questions we help to answer

  1. Do we comply with the GDPR in a demonstrable way?
  2. Do NIS2 or DORA apply to the company, directly or indirectly?
  3. What rules should guide the use of AI with personal data?
  4. Do our contracts with customers and processors protect us?

Typical deliverables

  • Record of processing activities
  • Revised clauses and contracts
  • Analysis of international transfers
  • AI use policy
  • Information security action plan
  • DORA diagnosis and action plan

What it includes

External DPO services
Record of processing activities
Contracts with customers and processors
International data transfers
Impact of AI on the GDPR
NIS2 requirements and information security
AI governance and ethics
Privacy and security training

Support for compliance with DORA (Digital Operational Resilience Act, Regulation (EU) 2022/2554), applicable since 17 January 2025 to financial entities and to third-party information and communication technology (ICT) service providers: diagnosis of applicability, ICT risk management, incident management and reporting, digital operational resilience testing, third-party risk management and the register of information on ICT contracts, in conjunction with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

Services  ·  05

A website that is found, understood and cited, in traditional search and in AI answers

Websites with SEO, GEO and AEO

We improve existing websites and build new ones on three fronts: technical performance (speed, accessibility and security), search engine optimisation (SEO) and readiness for artificial intelligence (AI) search: GEO (Generative Engine Optimisation), so that content is cited in AI-generated answers, and AEO (Answer Engine Optimisation), so that it answers searchers' questions directly.

Questions we help to answer

  1. Why doesn't the website show up when customers search for our services?
  2. Does the company appear in the answers of assistants such as ChatGPT, Gemini or Copilot?
  3. Is the website fast, accessible and easy to update?
  4. What enquiries and requests does the website bring in?

What it includes

Technical and content diagnosis
Search strategy based on customers' questions
Technical and content SEO
Structured data (schema.org)
GEO: factual, citable content, with authorship, dates and sources
AEO: direct answers to frequently asked questions
Creation of fast, accessible new websites
Measurement of results

Services  ·  06

AI training for those who use, apply and lead: open courses and tailored programmes

Training

Gadgetmetodo's training comes in two formats: the Generative AI at Work learning path, with five open six-hour courses, from introduction to implementing practical cases, and tailored programmes, such as AI Essentials, for boards and teams. The courses can also be run for a single company.

Questions we help to answer

  1. Which course should each person start with, and how far can they go?
  2. How do we move from individual use of AI to team processes?
  3. What rules and precautions are needed with data and outputs?

How it works

  • Online registration for each course; with no dates yet, registration for the course and notice of the class once dates are set
  • Diagnostic on registration: Gadgetmetodo recommends the right course for each trainee
  • Web page and PDF sheet for each course, with programme, practical cases and assessment
  • Documented evidence of the training completed

Engagement models and principles

Three ways of working with us, always with the terms written into the proposal

Fixed-scope project

Diagnosis, implementation of a solution or reorganisation of an area, with defined deliverables and payment tied to completion or to project milestones.

Ongoing support

Regular support for the board, for example as board adviser or external DPO, with periodic instalments, such as quarterly, over one or more years.

Training

Open courses in the Generative AI at Work learning path, with online registration, or the tailored AI Essentials programme, on their own or as part of a project.

Principles that guide our decisions

Clarity
Direct communication, understandable deliverables and well-founded decisions.
Method
Rigorous diagnostic, documented processes and indicators to measure progress.
Integration
People, processes and technology treated as a single system.
Trust
Confidentiality, data protection and common-sense governance.
